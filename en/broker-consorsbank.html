Our verdict

IN SHORT

Consorsbank is a classic universal bank with a securities account rather than a pure neobroker — that makes it more expensive per order than Trade Republic or Scalable Capital, but it also brings a broader product range (e.g. checking account, advice) if that matters to you.

Pros and cons

PROS

  • No custody fee
  • Broad product range beyond the brokerage account
  • Established full-service bank with a long history

CONS

  • Higher order fees than pure neobrokers
  • Higher savings-plan minimum (€10 instead of €1)

Who is Consorsbank a good fit for?

Well suited to anyone who wants a brokerage account alongside other classic banking products at an established provider, and who trades less often rather than saving tightly on a regular schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Is Consorsbank more expensive than Trade Republic?

For single orders, generally yes. For large order volumes the difference narrows somewhat, since the fee is also volume-based.

Do I need a checking account at Consorsbank for the brokerage account?

No, the brokerage account can also be opened independently of a checking account.
