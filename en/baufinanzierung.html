Mortgage broker comparison

Unlike a savings or current account, a mortgage has no single "best rate" — the actual interest rate depends on the loan-to-value ratio, creditworthiness, property type, region, the chosen fixed-rate period and repayment rate, and is calculated individually. So instead, we compare Germany's best-known independent brokers, who put your request in front of several hundred banks at once, by reach, locations and advisory offering.

Why don't we show comparison interest rates?

A mortgage interest rate is one of the most individually tailored financial products there is.

Loan-to-value ratio

The more equity you bring in, the lower the rate usually is. The difference between a 60% and a 90% loan-to-value ratio can change the rate significantly.

Creditworthiness & occupation

Your credit score, type of income (employed, self-employed, civil servant) and length of employment all feed into the bank's risk assessment.

Fixed-rate period & repayment rate

A longer fixed-rate period (e.g. 15 instead of 10 years) usually costs a premium but gives you more planning certainty. The repayment rate you choose affects the monthly payment and total term.

Property type & region

Existing vs. new build, location and the regional market also affect the terms a bank will offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is advice from a mortgage broker really free?

Yes, there's usually no cost to you as the customer. The broker instead earns a commission from the bank you end up financing with — regardless of whether you ultimately sign.

Should I ask several brokers at once?

That's generally possible and can help you compare terms. Just note that several inquiries in a short time can, in individual cases, be viewed critically by banks — check beforehand how the broker in question handles that.

Is my house bank or an independent broker better?

Your house bank already knows you but only offers its own rate. An independent broker compares several hundred banks at once — in practice, it's often worth checking both routes in parallel.
