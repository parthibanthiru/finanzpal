Frequently asked questions

Do I really need personal liability insurance?

It’s one of the few policies practically everyone should have — it protects you from damage you unintentionally cause to others, even if the amounts turn out to be very high. More in our guide article.

What should I check besides the price?

Above all the cover amount, whether key loss and uncollectible claims are included, and whether cover also applies abroad.

Can I switch at any time?

Usually yes, generally at the end of the contract term subject to a notice period — details are in your current contract.
