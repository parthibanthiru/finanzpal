Frequently asked questions

Credit card or debit card — what's the difference?

A credit card usually bundles your spending and settles it once a month, and is accepted more widely worldwide. A debit card charges your current account immediately. For travel and paying online abroad, a credit card is often the more practical choice.

What is a foreign transaction fee?

A percentage surcharge some banks charge when you pay or withdraw in a currency other than euros — often 1.5–2%. The best fee-free credit cards don't charge this at all.

Is there a catch with fee-free credit cards?

Being fee-free is often tied to conditions, such as a minimum monthly deposit or holding a particular current account with the same provider. Worth checking before you decide.
