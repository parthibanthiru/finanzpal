How is this calculated?

The calculator assumes monthly compounding and monthly deposits. The expected return is a simplified, constant annual rate — real markets naturally fluctuate from year to year. Taxes (capital gains tax, advance lump-sum tax) and product costs (TER) are not included in this simple version.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the result differ from other calculators?

Different calculators make slightly different assumptions (annual vs. monthly compounding, taxes yes/no). For a rough estimate, these differences are usually small.

Should I account for taxes?

Yes, for a more realistic net view you should factor in capital gains tax (a flat 25% plus solidarity surcharge, minus the tax-free allowance) separately — this calculator deliberately shows the gross growth.
