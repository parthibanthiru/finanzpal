Frühstart-Rente 2027: Subsidy, How It Works & Status

The Frühstart-Rente is a state-subsidized, capital-funded pension for children. The Bundestag passed the law on 16 October 2026 — Bundesrat approval is, as far as current reporting shows, still pending. This page tracks what's settled, how it works, and what's still open.

STATUS: PASSED BUNDESTAG, BUNDESRAT STILL PENDING

The Bundestag adopted the Frühstart-Rente law on 16 October 2026 in the version from the Finance Committee (CDU/CSU and SPD in favor, Greens and Left against, AfD abstaining). Whether and when Bundesrat approval is still required isn't clearly stated in reporting so far — we'll update this page once that's clarified.

Who benefits

Children aged 6 to 18. The subsidy starts with birth cohort 2020 — the children turning six in 2026. From 2027, each year's newly-turning-six cohort is added. Older children can still open a contract, but no longer receive the state subsidy.

Subsidy amount & cost to the state

€10 per month and child from the state, plus personal contributions of up to €6,840 per year are possible (from parents, grandparents, or others). The federal budget has allocated €198 million for 2027, rising to €411 million by 2030. The subsidized capital is, in principle, only paid out from age 65.

Two ways the money gets invested

YOUR OWN CONTRACT (STANDARDDEPOT)

  • Parents open a certified, cost-capped "Standarddepot" in the child's name with a provider of their choice
  • Can be set up fully digitally, with no advisory obligation
  • The provider applies for the subsidy automatically when the contract is signed (parents can object)
  • Reportedly a cost cap of around 1% p.a. in the government's draft

FALLBACK SOLUTION (NO CONTRACT OPENED)

  • If parents don't open a contract, the €10/month flows automatically into a federal special fund
  • Expected to be set up 1 January 2028, kept separate from the rest of the federal budget, not debt-financed
  • Managed by the Deutsche Bundesbank, invested exclusively in broadly diversified global equities or equity funds
  • No value guarantee — if markets fall, the state doesn't make up the difference
  • If a contract is opened later, the accumulated capital transfers over as a lump sum

Frequently asked questions

Has the Frühstart-Rente already been decided?

The Bundestag passed the law on 16 October 2026. Reporting so far doesn't clearly state whether and when Bundesrat approval is still needed — we'll update this page once that's clarified.

Do I need to do anything for my child myself?

No. If parents don't open their own contract, the subsidy flows automatically into the state fallback solution (a federal special fund managed by the Bundesbank). A contract of your own is an option, not a requirement.

Is the Frühstart-Rente the same as the Altersvorsorgedepot?

No, it's a separate product — a cost-capped "Standarddepot" specifically for children. Both are meant to launch on the same date (1 January 2027) and go through the same basic certification process, but are legally distinct.

Can I contribute extra myself?

Yes, up to €6,840 per calendar year — not just from parents, but also grandparents or others.

When is the money paid out?

Per the current status, the subsidized capital is, in principle, only paid out from age 65.
