Altersvorsorgedepot 2027: Status, Subsidies & Providers

The Altersvorsorgedepot replaces Germany's Riester pension. The underlying law has been in force since May 2026, and providers can launch from 1 January 2027 — but none is certified yet. This page tracks what's settled, what's still open, and who's already taking non-binding pre-registrations.

The Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) has been reviewing applications since 2026; as of 6 October 2026, reporting indicates no Altersvorsorgedepot had been certified yet. Cost figures some providers already publish are therefore provisional and subject to certification — we show them marked as such below, not as a comparison table of fixed numbers.

What the Altersvorsorgedepot changes

Unlike a Riester contract, the Altersvorsorgedepot allows free, return-oriented investment — in ETFs or funds, say — instead of a guarantee requirement. Existing Riester contracts continue unchanged with protection of existing rights; switching to the new product is voluntary and doesn't require repaying subsidies already received. New Riester contracts are no longer possible from 2027.

The subsidy, per the coalition agreement

Planned: a basic subsidy of up to €540 per year plus an additional child subsidy of €300 per year. Personal contributions are possible; ongoing product costs are meant to be capped. Payout and death-benefit rules for the retirement phase are reportedly still pending — the final implementing ordinance isn't finished yet.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Altersvorsorgedepot already law?

Yes. The Altersvorsorgereformgesetz has been in force since 30 May 2026 (Federal Law Gazette 2026 I No. 156). What's still open is the implementing ordinance with the detail rules, not the law itself.

Can I open an Altersvorsorgedepot already?

Not yet. Providers can launch at the earliest from 1 January 2027, and their product must first be certified by the Federal Central Tax Office — as of 6 October 2026 that hadn't happened for any provider.

What happens to my existing Riester contract?

It continues unchanged with protection of existing rights. Switching to the Altersvorsorgedepot is voluntary and doesn't require repaying subsidies already received. New Riester contracts, however, are no longer possible from 2027.

How large is the state subsidy?

Per the coalition agreement, up to €540 basic subsidy plus an additional €300 child subsidy per year. Final terms will only be settled with the implementing ordinance.
