Our verdict

IN SHORT

Trade Republic stands out mainly for combining a €0 custody fee, low €1 orders and free ETF savings plans from €1. Frequent traders with high order volume may find other brokers cheaper in individual cases — but for most beginners the fee advantage here is clear.

Pros and cons

PROS

  • No custody fee
  • ETF savings plans possible from €1
  • Simple, clear app
  • Fast account opening

CONS

  • No classic web login, app only
  • Limited fund selection
  • Phone support is limited

Who is Trade Republic a good fit for?

Trade Republic suits beginners especially well if they want to invest smaller amounts regularly in ETFs and value a simple app experience. If you actively trade many individual stocks or need a broad fund selection, consider a second broker alongside it.

Frequently asked questions

Is my money safe with Trade Republic?

Deposits are protected by law up to €100,000 per customer; securities are held as client assets separate from the broker’s own.

Can I transfer an existing savings plan?

Yes, a portfolio transfer from another broker is possible and usually free.

Is there a minimum deposit?

No, you can set up a savings plan from just €1 a month.
