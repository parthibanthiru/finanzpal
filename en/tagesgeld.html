Frequently asked questions

Is my money safe in an instant savings account?

Within the EU, deposits are protected by law up to €100,000 per customer and bank. For banks outside Germany, it’s worth checking the respective national protection scheme.

Why does the rate sometimes change?

Many providers adjust variable rates in line with general interest-rate movements. Accounts with a rate guarantee protect you from cuts for the stated period.

Instant savings account or fixed deposit — which is better?

An instant savings account stays available at any time; a fixed deposit usually ties up your money for a set period at a somewhat higher rate. The right choice depends on how quickly you need access. More in our guide article.
