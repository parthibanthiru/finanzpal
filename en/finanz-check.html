Finanz-Check: everything at once

Six quick questions — at the end you get a recommendation for your current account, custody account, credit card and overnight savings account all at the same time. Your answers aren't stored anywhere; every calculation runs directly in your browser using the same data as our individual comparisons.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a binding recommendation?

No. Finanz-Check is a quick orientation based on a handful of questions — for a well-founded decision, it's always worth checking the full comparison for each category, linked from every result card.

Where do the recommendations come from?

From the same datasets behind our current account, custody account, credit card and overnight savings comparisons — your answers award points per provider, and the provider with the most points wins each category.

Are my answers stored?

No. The quiz runs entirely in your browser; we don't store your answers or send them to a server.
