Frequently asked questions

Why is Trade Republic your recommendation?

For most beginners, the combination of low order fees, free savings plans and cash interest wins out. Anyone placing many individual orders per month, or needing an especially wide ETF range, may do better with other brokers in some cases — see the cards above.

Is it worth paying a custody fee?

Only if the conditions for a waiver (e.g. an active savings plan, a minimum number of trades) already apply to you, or additional services justify the price. For pure savings-plan investing, several permanently free alternatives exist.

Why do some brokers pay interest on my balance and others don't?

Brokers that offer their own savings or settlement account can often pay more attractive interest on uninvested cash than traditional banks. For some providers the rate isn't shown transparently, or is only a limited-time new-customer offer.

Does Finanzpal earn from your recommendation?

Yes, through partner links. The order follows our criteria, not the commission amount.

How often do you update the data?

At least quarterly, immediately for larger provider price changes.
