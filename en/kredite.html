How we compare

Loan rates depend heavily on individual creditworthiness — we therefore compare the effective annual rate for a representative example customer, as well as the terms that apply equally to all borrowers.

Effective rate

The actual annual rate including all costs, not just the advertised starting rate.

Flexibility

Free overpayment and the ability to adjust the installment if needed.

Transparency

How clearly costs and terms are communicated before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my rate differ from the advertised one?

The advertised “from” rate usually applies only to about two-thirds of customers with good creditworthiness. Your actual rate depends on your individual credit check.

How long does loan approval take?

With most providers compared here, a preliminary approval arrives within minutes; final payout takes 1–3 business days depending on the provider.

What does free overpayment mean?

You can repay additional amounts beyond the agreed installment without the provider charging an early-repayment penalty.
