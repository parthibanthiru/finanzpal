Net salary calculator

Work out your social contributions, income tax, and net pay from your gross salary — for all tax classes (I–IV, incl. the factor method), with child allowance, state and statutory or private health insurance.

YOUR INPUTS

Income tax is derived via a simplified annual calculation: instead of the official Vorsorgepauschale formula, we use the actual social/health insurance contributions paid — the official payroll tax program may give a slightly different result. Child allowances reduce (as in real payroll withholding) only the solidarity surcharge and church tax, not income tax itself — the full tax benefit only applies via your annual tax return. For a binding calculation, use a payslip or an official German wage/income tax calculator.

Tax class V/VI

Income tax for tax classes V and VI follows its own, multi-step calculation procedure (§39b(2) sentence 7 EStG) with a comparison calculation and a minimum-tax rule. We couldn't reconstruct the exact official formula with enough confidence, so we deliberately show no figure of our own here rather than risk a wrong result.

For tax class V/VI, please use the official BMF wage tax calculator.

How is this calculated?

Social contributions (pension, unemployment, health and long-term care insurance) are calculated using the 2026 contribution rates and contribution ceilings — long-term care insurance uses the higher employee share that applies in Saxony. With private health insurance, we use your own share after the statutory employer subsidy (at most half your premium, capped at half the statutory maximum contribution). Taxable income is derived, simplified, as annual gross salary minus these contributions, the standard employee allowance (€1,230), the standard special-expenses allowance (€36), and — for tax class II — the relief amount for single parents. The income tax tariff under §32a EStG is then applied — doubled for tax class III (splitting method), and for IV with factor multiplied by your factor afterwards. Child allowances only then reduce the assessment basis for the solidarity surcharge and church tax. Tax classes V and VI are not calculated (see above).

Frequently asked questions

Why does this differ from my real payslip?

Your payslip uses the official Vorsorgepauschale formula instead of your actual contributions, and may account for further registered allowances — this calculator models the most common case, simplified.

Why don't child allowances reduce my income tax?

That's how real payroll withholding works too: child allowances only affect the solidarity surcharge and church tax each month. The actual tax benefit from child allowances only applies once the tax office checks it as part of your annual tax return (a comparison against child benefit already received).

Why do I pay little or no solidarity surcharge despite a high income?

Since the 2021 reform, the solidarity surcharge no longer applies to most taxpayers — it only kicks in above an annual income tax of around €20,350 (or €40,700 for tax class III), and is further capped in a "phase-in zone" just above that threshold.

What is the health insurance Zusatzbeitrag?

Every statutory health insurer sets its own additional contribution on top of the nationwide base rate of 14.6%, split evenly between you and your employer. The 2026 average is 2.9% — check your health insurer's membership certificate for the exact figure.

How is the employer subsidy for private health insurance calculated?

Your employer covers half your PKV premium, but at most half of what they'd pay for a statutorily insured employee on the same salary (capped at the contribution ceiling).
