Work out your social contributions, income tax, and net pay from your gross salary — with every step broken out individually.

YOUR INPUTS

This calculator models tax class I (single, no children, no further allowances) — for married people (tax class III/IV/V) or with children, the result will differ. Income tax is derived via a simplified annual calculation: instead of the official Vorsorgepauschale formula, we use the actual social contributions paid — the official payroll tax program may give a slightly different result. For a binding calculation, use a payslip or an official German wage/income tax calculator.

How is this calculated?

Social contributions (pension, unemployment, health and long-term care insurance) are calculated using the 2026 contribution rates and contribution ceilings. Taxable income is derived, simplified, as annual gross salary minus social contributions, the standard employee allowance (€1,230) and the standard special-expenses allowance (€36). The income tax tariff under §32a EStG (basic table) is then applied, followed by the solidarity surcharge (with its exemption threshold and phase-in zone) and, optionally, church tax.

Frequently asked questions

Why does this differ from my real payslip?

Your payslip also accounts for your exact tax class, any registered allowances, child allowances, and the official Vorsorgepauschale formula — this calculator deliberately models only the most common case (tax class I, no children), simplified.

Why do I pay little or no solidarity surcharge despite a high income?

Since the 2021 reform, the solidarity surcharge no longer applies to most taxpayers — it only kicks in above an annual income tax of around €20,350 (tax class I), and is further capped in a "phase-in zone" just above that threshold.

What is the health insurance Zusatzbeitrag?

Every statutory health insurer sets its own additional contribution on top of the nationwide base rate of 14.6%, split evenly between you and your employer. The 2026 average is 2.9% — check your health insurer's membership certificate for the exact figure.
