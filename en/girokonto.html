What to look for when choosing a checking account

These criteria help you find the right account for everyday use.

Account fee

Many banks only waive the fee with a minimum monthly incoming payment or for younger account holders — check whether you meet the condition.

Overdraft rate

The interest rate for overdrawing your account. An overdraft should only be used for short gaps, since the rate is usually well above that of an installment loan.

Cards and cash withdrawal

Not every account has a classic credit card — many modern accounts rely on a free debit card instead. How often you can withdraw cash for free varies widely.

Deposit protection

Within the EU, deposits are legally protected up to €100,000 per customer and bank — this also applies to banks based in another EU country.

Frequently asked questions

Debit card or credit card — what's the difference?

A debit card charges transactions directly to your account; a credit card usually bundles them and settles once a month. Many modern checking accounts now rely on a free Visa or Mastercard debit card instead of a classic credit card.

Is my money safe with a neobank?

Banks with their own banking license (e.g. N26, Trade Republic, C24) are subject to the same statutory deposit protection as traditional banks — up to €100,000 per customer.

Is it worth paying for an account?

Only if the extras (e.g. a branch network, bonus program, personal advice) are worth the price to you. For plain account-keeping, there are now many permanently free alternatives.
