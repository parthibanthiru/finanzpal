What to look for when choosing tax software

These criteria help you find the right program for your situation.

Target group

Some programs only cover employees, others also handle rental income, investment income or self-employment. Check in advance whether your situation is supported.

Price and payment timing

Many providers only charge when you actually submit your tax return — so you can estimate your refund beforehand without commitment.

ELSTER connection

Most programs file directly through the ELSTER interface. Whether you need your own ELSTER certificate for that varies by provider.

Platform

Pure web programs run in the browser; others also offer apps or installable desktop software.

Frequently asked questions

Is it even worth filing a tax return?

For most employees, yes: the Federal Fiscal Court (Bundesfinanzhof) estimates an average refund in the three-digit range. A voluntary tax return can be filed retroactively for up to four years.

Tax app or classic software — what's the difference?

Apps like Taxfix or Steuerbot guide you through the return with a question dialog and usually suit simpler cases. Classic software like WISO Steuer often offers more depth for more complex cases such as rental income or self-employment.

Do I need my own ELSTER certificate?

That depends on the program — some file without one, for others it's unclear or required. You'll find details in the expandable program view above.

Isn't ELSTER free?

Yes, the tax office provides ELSTER as a free portal for filing your return. Tax software usually costs extra, but in exchange offers guided data entry, plausibility checks, and often a higher refund through automatically identified deductions.
