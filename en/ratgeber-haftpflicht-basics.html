Protecting

Personal liability insurance: what actually matters

It’s one of the few policies practically everyone needs — but not every plan covers the same thing.

JM
Julia Mertens
Updated October 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Personal liability insurance steps in when you accidentally cause damage to someone else — whether a broken vase during a visit or a serious bicycle accident. Especially with injury to people, the sums can quickly run into the hundreds of thousands, which is why this cover matters so much.

1. Pay attention to the cover amount

Many older or very cheap plans still offer cover of only €3–5 million. Current recommendations are more like at least €10 million, ideally €30–50 million — the price difference between these tiers is usually small, but the difference in a worst case is substantial.

2. Benefits that are often worth it

  • Uncollectible-claims cover: Steps in when someone else causes you damage but can’t pay themselves.
  • Key loss: Covers the cost if you lose someone else’s key (e.g. for a rented apartment).
  • Worldwide cover: Important if you travel abroad often — some budget plans only cover within Europe.
GOOD TO KNOW

Couples or families can often use a single joint policy instead of several individual ones — that can save money, but it should be explicitly provided for in the contract.

3. Typical exclusions in the fine print

Damage you cause intentionally is never covered, as a matter of principle. Depending on the plan, certain activities (e.g. volunteer club work, renting out items) may also be excluded — it’s worth checking the policy terms before signing up.
