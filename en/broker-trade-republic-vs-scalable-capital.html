Strengths at a glance

TRADE REPUBLIC

  • One single, consistently simple pricing model — no tier decision needed
  • Low order commission even without a paid tier
  • Portfolio transfer also free

SCALABLE CAPITAL

  • Larger ETF savings-plan universe (3,212 vs. 2,827)
  • Slightly higher cash interest (2.60% vs. 2.50%)
  • Standalone web login possible — no paired app needed to sign in
  • With Prime+ (€4.99/month), single orders of €250 or more without order fee, plus higher deposit protection (up to €500,000)

Which broker fits whom?

Trade Republic is a good fit if you prefer a single, easy-to-understand pricing model with no tier to choose, and only save occasionally or in smaller amounts; signing in always goes through the app, even if you then continue in the browser. Scalable Capital suits anyone who wants a larger choice of ETF savings plans or prefers to sign in without a smartphone app at all — the no-cost Free tier is enough for that. Prime+ only pays off with regular single orders of €250 or more (from about six a month). Neither broker charges a custody fee, requires a minimum deposit, or charges for savings-plan executions.

Frequently asked questions

Which broker is cheaper for small savings amounts?

For savings plans there is no difference: both execute ETF savings plans from €1 without an order fee. They only differ slightly for single orders — Trade Republic €1.00, Scalable Capital €0.99 under Free.

Is Scalable Capital's Prime+ worth it over Trade Republic?

Not for savings plans — those are free at both. Prime+ (€4.99/month) only pays off if you regularly place single orders of €250 or more: from about five such orders a month compared with Trade Republic's €1.00 per order (5 × €1.00 = €5.00). More detail on the Scalable Capital review page.

Can I switch brokers later?

Yes, a portfolio transfer is possible with both providers and is usually free.
