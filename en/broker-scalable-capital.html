Our verdict

IN SHORT

Scalable Capital splits into two models: Free (€0.99 per order) and Prime+ (€4.99/month, with flat-rate savings plans across a large ETF universe). If you save into several ETFs at once regularly, Prime+ often pays off quickly; for occasional single purchases, Free is enough.

Pros and cons

PROS

  • Two pricing models for different saving intensity
  • Large savings-plan universe with Prime+
  • Web and app access (not app-only)

CONS

  • Without Prime+, many savings plans incur per-execution fees
  • Pricing model needs more explaining than competitors’

Who is Scalable Capital a good fit for?

Especially for anyone running several ETF savings plans in parallel, who can amortize the Prime+ flat rate quickly as a result. Occasional traders are usually better off with Free than Prime+.

Frequently asked questions

Is Prime+ worth it for me?

Roughly: from about 2–3 savings plans of €50–100 a month each, Prime+ often pays off versus Free within the first year.

Can I switch between the models?

Yes, switching between Free and Prime+ is generally possible at any time in your account.
