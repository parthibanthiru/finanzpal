How is this calculated?

The calculator uses the standard annuity formula: the monthly payment stays constant over the term, the interest portion falls with each payment (as the remaining balance falls) and the principal portion rises accordingly — so the loan is fully repaid at the end of the stated term.

Frequently asked questions

What is the effective annual rate?

The effective annual rate (APR) includes fees and the payment schedule on top of the plain nominal rate — it's the more realistic figure for comparing loan offers, and the value you should enter here.

Can I repay the loan early?

Most German installment loans allow free or low-cost early repayment, though exact terms vary by provider. This calculator models regular, constant payments without any extra repayment.

Which provider currently has the best terms?

That depends on the loan amount, term and your creditworthiness. See our loan comparison for current rates across providers.
