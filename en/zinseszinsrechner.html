How is this calculated?

The calculator assumes monthly compounding: each month your balance grows by 1/12 of the entered annual rate, and any extra monthly deposit is added afterwards. This is the classic compound-interest formula, applied to monthly rather than annual periods.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference from simple interest?

With simple interest, you earn interest only on your starting balance each year. With compound interest, you also earn interest on the interest already credited in previous years — so the balance grows faster and faster over time.

What kind of investment is this calculator for?

It works for anything with a fixed or assumed annual rate — e.g. a savings account, a fixed-term deposit, or a simplified estimate for a securities investment. For an ETF savings plan with broker guidance, use our savings plan calculator instead.
