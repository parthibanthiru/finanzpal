How is this calculated?

During the savings phase, your capital grows through monthly contributions and compound interest. At retirement, this is converted into a level monthly pension (annuity formula) limited to your chosen payout duration, which gradually draws down the remaining balance while it continues to earn interest. Taxes and product costs are not included in this simplified version.

Germany's new retirement savings account (AVD)

What is the Altersvorsorgedepot?

From 1 January 2027, the Altersvorsorgedepot is planned to replace the previous Riester pension as Germany's state-subsidized private retirement product. Unlike many existing Riester contracts, it allows free, return-oriented investing — e.g. in ETFs — combined with government subsidies.

What subsidies are planned?

A base subsidy of 50 cents per euro contributed up to an annual cap is planned, plus an additional 25 cents per euro for amounts above that up to a further limit. The exact amounts and conditions will be set by the final legislation.

Is there a cost cap?

Yes, a cap on ongoing product costs of around 1% per year is planned, so that more of the subsidy and returns reach the saver.

Why doesn't this calculator factor in the subsidy precisely?

The exact subsidy and taxation rules (base subsidy, child subsidy, career-starter bonus, special expense deduction, more-favorable-treatment test, deferred taxation) were not yet fully finalized at the time this calculator was built. Rather than present uncertain assumptions as precise numbers, we show you the well-established savings and payout math here — and will update this page with the confirmed subsidy details once they are settled.
